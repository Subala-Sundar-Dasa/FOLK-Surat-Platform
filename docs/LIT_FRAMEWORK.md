# LIT framework

LIT is a private 1-5 assessment on three independent axes:

- **L — Learning/relationship receptivity:** humility, openness, willingness to hear, accept correction, and follow appropriate guidance.
- **I — Interest:** interest in Krishna consciousness, philosophy, and God consciousness.
- **T — Time:** demonstrated willingness and practical capacity to give time.

There are 125 coordinate combinations. Do not flatten them into a single score. Show a 3D coordinate/history visualization and generate an axis-specific relationship strategy. Equal totals can represent very different people.

Reassess after roughly three meaningful interactions rather than on a rigid monthly cycle. Physical personal meetings carry the strongest evidence, followed by video calls, phone calls, service discussions, post-program conversations, and substantial WhatsApp exchanges. The essence of association evidence is the degree of comfortable, meaningful personal revelation—not mere attendance.

Store assessor, evidence, confidence, and temporary circumstances. Treat predictions as hypotheses for guide review, never as diagnoses or deterministic personality labels.
