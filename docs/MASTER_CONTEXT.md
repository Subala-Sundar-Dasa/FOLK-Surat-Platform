# Master product context

FOLK Surat is a private, mobile-first relationship and spiritual-growth platform serving students connected through SVNIT, IIIT Surat, and local Surat colleges. It combines a student experience, a guide experience, a web CRM, planning and reminders, quarterly progress reviews, and a Prabhupada-grounded knowledge layer.

## Product intent

The system should help guides build real relationships, notice what matters, and decide the next personal action without forcing them to fill dozens of fields. Guides may dictate verbose interaction notes; the system extracts a draft structured summary, asks only for important missing information, and retains the mentor-approved summary.

The student app, named **FOLK Surat**, is a one-stop ABCDE experience: association, books, chanting, devotional service, and expeditions. The guide app retains and improves daily sadhana monitoring. The web app provides CRM administration, planning, analytics, content, and cross-cohort oversight.

## Core philosophy

- LIT is a private prioritization and relationship-assessment framework.
- ABCDE describes observable activities and growth. It is independent from LIT.
- AI supports observation, retrieval, summarization, reminders, and options. It does not make pastoral decisions.
- Prabhupada supplies principle-level grounding; practical personal guidance comes through the FOLK guide.
- Quality and sincere engagement matter more than mass participation.

## Data-entry principle

Natural language first, structured fields second. The system should infer candidate values with evidence and confidence, then ask one targeted follow-up only when a missing fact would materially improve action. Student reflection can come through a friendly private link or be entered by a guide after a WhatsApp conversation.
