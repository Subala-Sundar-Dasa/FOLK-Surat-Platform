# Roles, approvals, security, and privacy

## Authority

- Student: own sadhana, reflections, bookings, content progress, service interest, trips/competitions.
- Guide: assigned students, interaction summaries, recommendations, trip/competition planning, operational changes.
- HOD: complete Surat visibility, assignments, and approval of foundational goals or quarterly-target changes.

Discounts, consequential recommendations, and changes inferred from student chat require guide approval. Calendar changes reported in chat become proposed updates until a guide approves them.

## Disclosure and pastoral safety

Student chat is part of the guide-supported system. Present a concise one-time disclosure that relevant conversations may be visible to the assigned guide; do not repeat it so often that natural conversation is obstructed. The system is not a confidential therapist or independent spiritual authority. Escalate urgent safety concerns through a separately approved protocol.

## Repository safety

Private repository only. Forbidden: secrets, OAuth client secrets, service-role keys, signing keys, real-student data, production exports, unrelated personal files, or paid/external processing without approval. Use synthetic fixtures. Run secret and large-file scans before every corpus update.
