# RDUA, trips, content, and competitions

## RDUA

RDUA means Read, Discuss, Understand, Analyze. The planned source has 20 topics with 10 paragraphs each. Split each topic into two sessions of five paragraphs, yielding 40 sessions; attach three reflection questions to each session.

First-year hostel sessions may include one round of collective chanting, discussion, and prasadam under a sincere/eager student leader. Online leaders should normally have meaningful history, at least four small Prabhupada books completed, and credible chanting/reading practice; final appointment remains guide judgment. Daily is the aspiration, but attendance thresholds and interventions remain guide-discretionary.

## Trips

Each trip records theme, collective objectives, participant list, each participant's starting position, intended next position, connection/bonding with the guide, end commitment, later follow-through, and post-trip eagerness (`positive`, `neutral`, `negative`). Attendance alone is not spiritual progress. Guides may plan trips freely while keeping HOD informed; foundational strategy changes require HOD approval.

Initial expedition ideas include Ahmedabad/Radhastami, a first-year Ahmedabad trip after midterms, Vrindavan after Navaratri in Kartik, Dwarka after exams, Mumbai around late January/early February, and a central Ahmedabad-led trip in early March. Exact dates must come from approved academic calendars.

## Competitions and services

Competition builders must allow theme, end goal, emphasized behaviors, scoring, eligibility, rewards, approval, and web/mobile participation. Discount recommendations may consider approved activity signals but require guide approval. Service opportunities support both listed needs and student-proposed skills, with guide conversation rather than burdensome forms.
