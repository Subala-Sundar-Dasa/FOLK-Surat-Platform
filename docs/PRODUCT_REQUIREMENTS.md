# Product requirements

## Student app: FOLK Surat

- Google-only sign-in.
- Preserve and improve daily sadhana entry.
- ABCDE home experience with podcasts, hearing assignments, embedded reading, chanting counter, service opportunities, trips, competitions, reflections, and mentor appointment requests.
- Personalized hearing and reading recommendations based on progress and incomplete chapters.
- Book-interest prompts may use the next three chapters to create curiosity without fabricating content.
- In-app content viewing should avoid repeated external browser tabs. Remote streaming keeps the application lightweight.
- Student-facing Prabhupada-grounded encouragement and optional short conversation.
- When discussion becomes personal or practical, encourage direct guide contact and alert the assigned guide under the documented disclosure policy.
- Target eventual release size: 30-35 MB; debug APK size is not a release-size measure.

## Guide app

- Preserve and improve current sadhana monitoring and notifications.
- Daily actionable queue, overdue association, birthdays, upcoming availability, trips, commitments, missing sadhana, and suggested next actions.
- Voice/text interaction note -> extracted draft -> targeted missing question -> mentor approval -> retained summary.
- Student assignment, cohort/college/calendar context, LIT history, ABCDE details, and evidence/confidence.
- Weekly Monday dashboard: quarterly position, next-week target, realistic actions, and priority students.
- HOD receives visibility into material developments without becoming an approval bottleneck.

## Web CRM

- Surat-wide aggregate with SVNIT, IIIT, local-college, college, year, and academic-calendar filters.
- Quarterly target vs actual vs remaining dashboards.
- Student profile timeline and next-best-action workspace.
- Content, RDUA, trips, competitions, calendars, service opportunities, and approvals.
- Foundational target changes require HOD approval.

## Notifications

- Example configurable sadhana schedule: 08:00 all students; 11:00 non-submitters; 14:00 guide report; 14:00 or 17:00 final non-submitter reminder.
- Birthday reminders one day before and on the day, with an editable Krishna-conscious WhatsApp greeting under 50 words.
- Calendar-aware slowing before exams/festivals and renewed activity after them.
- Notifications must be useful, frequency-capped, snoozable where appropriate, and never harassing.
