# Adaptive Vocabulary 1.0

Vocabulary 1.0 is specific to spoken conversations, lectures, ceremonies, interviews, and philosophical discussions. It is not inherited from the Dham Yatra or books corpus.

## Operational rules

1. Use 8–15 dominant source tags and 3–8 event tags.
2. Rank by density and corpus distinctiveness, not mere occurrence.
3. Keep event form, scriptural anchor, philosophical stance, personal dealing, instruction scope, and transcript certainty as separate dimensions.
4. Do not treat quoted or refuted positions as Śrīla Prabhupāda's conclusion.
5. Preserve historical context and never invent unclear audio.
