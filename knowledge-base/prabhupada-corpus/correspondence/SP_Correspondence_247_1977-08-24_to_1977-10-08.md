### Correspondence text

My dear Tusta Krishna Maharaja,
Please accept my obeisances. I have been instructed by His Divine Grace Srila Prabhupada to thank you for your letter dated 7th August, 1977.
Srila Prabhupada heard your letter with great attention, and looked very carefully at the photographs which you have sent of the temple which you are constructing. Srila Prabhupada was very glad to see the progress that has been made thus far, and wishes you to keep Him supplied with photographs until the building is actually finished, His Divine Grace appreciated the nice arches over the door and windows and said that it will look very nice when completed. He was very glad that all of the work has been done by our own workmen, and said , “That is our credit.” His Divine Grace also heard the newspaper clippings which you enclosed and appreciated how you were preaching very nicely.
One point has come up a number of times, and most recently in a letter from Harisauri. I know that some time you have differences of opinions with your Godbrothers. It appears that sometimes you criticize the devotees for their book distribution techniques. Srila Prabhupada wanted me to write you the following which is directly taken down from His Divine Grace’s statement: “If you don’t like the way they are doing distribution, you do better. Show the example. Whatever is in their power they are doing. Every salesman has his own tactics and the result is judged by how much one sells. The end justifies the means. Why are you criticizing publicly the devotees in this way? They are selling books on my order. Why are you criticizing? That means indirectly you are criticizing me.” I pray that you will try to understand that it is only on Srila Prabhupada’s orders that devotees are distributing so vigorously, and that His Divine Grace is very much aware of the manner in which they are distributing. But according to His vision, which is as close to Krishna’s vision as anyone could be, it is being done in the proper way according to time, place, and circumstances, and considering that the end justifies the means.
In your letter you have stated that devotees are doing 24 hour kirtan for Srila Prabhupada’s recovery. When His Divine Grace heard this He said, “That is the only way I will recover.” You will be glad to know that Srila Prabhupada has decided to travel around the world again. He is leaving on Saturday morning for London. He says that by the loving attention of His devotees He will certainly recover. Please pray fervently for His complete and quick cure.
Hoping this meets you in the best of health and Krishna consciousness.
Your servant,
Tamal Krishna Gosvami
Secretary to Srila Prabhupada
His Holiness Tusta Krishna Swami
Hare Krishna Marae,
Muriwai Valley Rd.
R.D. 1. Waimauku
Ph. Waimauku 2D, New Zealand

### Correspondence text

My dear Giriraj Prabhu,
Please accept my humble obeisances at your feet. I beg to acknowledge receipt of your letters dated 4th, 18th, 19th August, 1977, respectively.
By the time you receive this letter Srila Prabhupada should have already left India. I could not inform you earlier of His Divine Grace’s plans, because Srila Prabhupada was thinking to go one day and the not to go the next. It was only three days ago that His Divine Grace actually decided definitely to leave India. His reason is that He feels the change will be good for His health, as will a cooler climate. But most of all He wants to be in His western temples with the western disciples because He feels that this will enthuse Him and give Him new life. Srila Prabhupada says that He wants to see our society a little stronger, and by His going around the world, even if He does not speak but is simply present, this will make our society stronger and it will thus endure permanently. So now once again you will have to serve His Divine Grace in separation.
We have just received the copy of the bank advice debiting our Mayapur Vrndavana Fund account for Rs.6,00,000/-. However there has been no advice sent to us regarding the transfer which must have been received by the bank in order for this money to be debited. Where is that advice? Also as you have written in your letter, the Fixed Deposit receipt for Rs.18,932/75 has been deposited in the BBT account. But we have not received an advice from the bank in this regard. Could you please send a copy of this advice? Now that we are going to be outside of India, please continue to send these advices, as it is the only way we can keep the accounts up to date in our books.
The matter of the transfers from Central Bank of India to Syndicate Bank of Vrndavana has now been cleared up. By our careful study we have found that Rs. 250 was not credited to our account by Syndicate Bank but instead they credited it to another one of their accounts about ten months ago. So they have now credited our account this amount plus interest. Thank you for your helping on your end.
Regarding the chant, you should chant Jaya sri krsna caitanya prabhu nityananda jayadvaita gadadhara srivasadi gour bhakta vrnda, or sri krsna caitanya prabhu nityananda sri advaitagadadhara srilvasadi gour bhakta vrnda. Either sri or jaya can be chanted, but not bhaja.
I read your letter dated 5th June, 1977, which you had begun writing to His Divine Grace, to Srila Prabhupada. Srila Prabhupada heard your unfinished letter, with full sympathy. When you inquired from Him whether the feelings which you are now experiencing are substantial or sentimental, Prabhupada said: “May Krishna bless you. These are all the blessings of Krishna. They are not ordinary.”
Regarding the balance Rs.10.00.000/- which was to be given in installments, I have just received a letter from Ramesvara Maharaja, a section of which is copied and enclosed herewith. As you can see from Ramesvara Maharaja’s letter, my original letter was quite accurate. Ramesvara Maharaja never promised Rs.42,00,000/-, but only promised to send $280,000. Furthermore he finds it quite impossible to start repaying any loans as there are so many expenses for printing, and besides this, he does not feel nor do the general devotees in America feel, that they want to donate any more money to Bombay. When Ramesvara Maharaja’s letter arrived, it was read to His Divine Grace, with Satsvarupa Maharaja, Balavanta Prabhu, Guru Krpa Maharaja, and myself all present. Srila Prabhupada agreed with Ramesvara Maharaja that no more money should be donated to Bombay from foreign sources beyond the’ payments which he has already committed. Srila Prabhupada said that this loan is not possible. This I had actually suspected from the beginning, that Ramesvara would not be able to repay this loan. So now it will be up to all of you to collect the money to complete the construction. As His Divine Grace will not be present at the opening ceremony, which must still go on as scheduled, then you can finish the construction after it opens. Now you will have to plan having Rs.16,00,000/- less to spend. I am enclosing a Loan Agreement* for the Rs.6,00,000/-, already loaned, which you will kindly sign and return to us. I know this must be a little depressing, but I do not think actually that it is unwarranted. This is the first project that has been totally built by foreign funds. Practically nothing has been collected locally, either to purchase the land or to build the temple or guesthouse. You are all in the richest city in India and one of the richest cities in the world, so for you to have to raise Rs. 16,00,000/- towards the construction is not at all improper. Actually I have to say as one of the three trustees of Bombay, that I think the money should be collected in Bombay (this Rs. 16,00,000/-.) There is no end to how much money you will want from foreign countries, as we have seen in the past, and Srila Prabhupada was very happy to receive this letter from Ramesvara Maharaja so that He could take the opportunity to say: No more funds. If you sell the rooms, that will be a good source of income for finishing the construction. And I am certain that there are other men who will also be able to donate towards this end.
Srila Prabhupada always keeps you within His heart, and I know how much He appreciates hearing from you as often as you like to write. So while He is overseas I request that you write very often, as Prabhupada is always thinking of Bombay. The other day Srila Prabhupada said, “I was thinking why I have neglected the West for so long. It is because I had to persist in Bombay.” Actually for the last six or seven years Srila Prabhupada’s main work besides translating has been the Bombay temple. So you must keep Him continuously informed of all the advents and activities, with full descriptions, photographs, and anything else you can send which will allow Srila Prabhupada to see through your eyes.
I pray that this meets you in ever-increasing Krishna consciousness.
Your servant,
Tamal Krishna Gosvami
Secretary to Srila Prabhupada

P.S. I am sending the secretary’s desk key with Gopal Krishna as he wants to get some books to take to the Russian bookfare. After he gets what he needs, kindly take the key and keep it carefully locked in your safe. I am also sending some files of Srila Prabhupada’s correspondence to Aditya. Srila Prabhupada’s correspondence from previous months has been kept in one of the cabinets in the secretary’s library. That is one of the low wooden cabinets. So she should put these further correspondence in that cabinet, lock it up, and return the key to you. The key f or all of these cabinets is kept in my desk drawer. So the key that you are getting is the desk draw key. After she uses the cabinets, and Gopal uses the cabinets to get his books, the keys to the cabinets should be put back in the secretary’s desk drawer, locked up, and the key for the drawer returned to you to be kept in the safe.
His Grace Giriraj das Brahmachary
c/o ISKCON Bombay

### Correspondence text

My dear Hridayananda Maharaj,
Please accept my most humble obeisances at your feet. I have been instructed by His Divine Grace Srila Prabhupada to thank you for your letter dated August 7th, 1977.
Srila Prabhupada heard your short but enthusing report along with the other devotees assembled here, Satsvarupa Maharaja, Balavanta, Guru Krpa Maharaja, and Madhudvisa (who has just returned). His Divine Grace was very much encouraged to hear of the preaching activities of your zone. Regarding the big mountain peak in Rio which has a carved face, Srila Prabhupada says that this must be Ravana’s Tunnel. His Divine Grace said that He felt that you are right that it is formerly the capital of the kingdom of Ravana.
Srila Prabhupada has made the journey safely from India to West. Srila Prabhupada is very much enjoying His stay here in Bhaktivedanta Manor, which He says is the nicest house He has in the world. It is very peaceful and quiet and Srila Prabhupada is resting more deeply here than He has done in all the months in Vrndavana. He is quite happy now to be back in the West amongst His so many loving devotees. Tentatively Srila Prabhupada plans to stay here and afterward go on to New York, then the New York farm, and probably from there to Los Angeles. The exact timings of these movements has not yet been defined. Bhagavan Prabhu is here, and Guru Krpa Maharaja is here, and Satsvarupa and Balavanta were just with Srila Prabhupada in Vrndavana. Any time you would like to spend a month with His Divine Grace, I’m sure Srila Prabhupada would very much enjoy having you. The GBC men who stay get to serve Srila Prabhupada very personally by massaging Him and performing other nectarean services.
I pray that this finds you and all the devotees in your zone in the best of health and Krishna consciousness.
Your servant,
Tamal Krishna Gosvami
Secretary to Srila Prabhupada
His Holiness Hridayananda das Gosvami
c/o ISKCON Sao Paulo

My dear God-brothers and God-sisters of New Dvaraka-dhama,
Kindly accept my humble obeisances at your feet. I have been instructed by His Divine Grace Srila Prabhupada to extend His thanks to all of you for performing the ceremony of Rathayatra of Lord Jagannatha so wonderfully. Srila Prabhupada has received your loving offering of Guru-Daksina, as well as the album of photographs depicting the Rathayatra held recently in Los Angeles. Just this morning when Srila Prabhupada arose from bed, He spoke to me about how He had held Rathayatra early in His childhood. He recounted as follows: “When I was five or six years old, any carpenter I saw I would ask him why don’t you make a Rathayatra cart for me. ‘Yes, why not,’ they would say. I would have my father take me to the carpenter shop, but none of them would make it. One old lady saw me crying on the street because I could not have the Rathayatra cart built. She asked my father why is he crying and my father explained because he wants a Rathayatra cart but no carpenter is making it. I have got one, said the old lady, and my father purchased it from her. He bought oil paint for me and I painted. I was also preparing fireworks for the occasion. I saw little books how to do it. My father gave me anything I wanted and thus put my mother into anxiety. And I was very persistent. All day friends and children would help me and I was never lazy. From: my birth I was never lazy neither dull. Neither was I ever defeated by anyone. Whatever I would order to my parents or friends must be obeyed. Krishna has given me always leadership. How I a poor Indian, have leadership of the Europeans and Americans? And amongst the authors of the world I am also the leader.”
Now the photographs depicting Rathayatra in Los Angeles arrived from you. His Divine Grace was fully entranced on how all of you, His loving disciples and children, dedicated all of your energy in this ceremony. With keen interest Srila Prabhupada heard the description of each photograph and glanced so lovingly at each of you who were engaged in the service of Lord Jagannatha. There were tears in Srila Prabhupada’s eyes and they made a path down His cheeks as He shook His head from side to side, so much appreciative was He of the effort which you have all made. When the photographs showing the three Chariots on procession were shown to Him, Srila Prabhupada made a long humming sound, and with His eyebrows very arched He declared, “We have never seen such carts!” When the photographs showing the long lines of people lining up to see the “Changing Body” exhibit were shown to Him, Srila Prabhupada was deeply pleased and said, “I told you this would happen. I am very much glad to see this.” The experience of seeing these photographs was so much moving to His Divine Grace that He was unable to sit for His massage, nor could he take His meals. We requested Him to have His massage, but in a choked voice, He said not now. Then after two hours He retired to bed, deeply in trance meditating on the Rathayatra of Lord Jagannatha at Los Angeles.
You are all so much sincere and beloved to His Divine Grace and by your prayers Srila Prabhupada is living. Lord Krishna will surely bring His Pure Devotee to you to stay with you in New Dvaraka and there will be no limit to the ecstasy we will all feel everyday when we can sing and dance before our Spiritual Master, and praise His glorious activities.
The prayers which you have repeatedly offered in your letter, dedicating your lives to the publication and distribution of Srila Prabhupada’s books, moved His Divine Grace so much that He was crying and could not speak. Your presentation of the 1st volume of the Tenth Canto to Him was so much appreciated by Him, and He is having us read it on the Supreme Lord’s Appearance Day. When Srila Prabhupada beheld the beautiful offering of the Vyasa Puja Book, He turned through each of the pages and afterwards said, “Like father like son.” You are all so much fortunate to be residing in New Dvaraka because Prabhupada is always remembering New Dvarka and all of you who are there.
Your servant,
Tamal Krishna Goswami
Secretary to Srila Prabhupada

Dear Mrs. Perlmutter,
Please accept my greetings. Hare Krishna. I beg to thank you for your letter dated Sept. 6, 1977 with the enclosed news clipping from the New York Times.
Yes, we were expecting to take Srila Prabhupada to America, but as Krishna desired Srila Prabhupada returned to India after a two week visit to London. Here His Divine Grace is lying ill, but waiting for the opening of the beautiful International Temple and Guesthouse at Hare Krishna Land, Juhu, Bombay. Sometime in the future I hope that you will the opportunity to visit India and see all these tremendous ISKCON projects which Srila Prabhupada has developed here. There are very breath-taking and inspiring and I am sure you will be very happy to see them. I think in the future you and your good husband should plan a trip here. It would be a most enlightening experience.
You will be glad to know that the Bhaktivedanta Book Trust Exhibition at the Moscow Fair was a grand success. Many of the biggest libraries in Russia ordered standing orders of all of Srila Prabhupada’s books. Among those which took our books were: The Moscow University, The Lenin Library, The Library for Foreign Literature, The Moscow Historical Library, and the Moscow Museum for Oriental Arts. Thousands of people mobbed our exhibit of books, and they were so eager to get the books that many of them were even stolen. So this is real proof that Krishna consciousness is universal. It cuts through all barriers and designations - religion, nationality, and even philosophy. Krishna consciousness is the true eternal nature of all living entities. It is not something artificial which might be accepted or rejected. It is the natural consciousness of the pure spirit soul, and as such we always remain enthusiastic to explain Krishna consciousness to all people whom we meet, knowing fully well that they will actually be happy if they take to Krishna consciousness.
I hope this will meet you in enthusiastic preaching mood to try to explain to all of the parents of our devotees that they should be happy to have their sons and daughters as Hare Krishna devotees. Please offer my greetings to your husband.
Your sincerely,
Tamal Krishna Goswami
Secretary to His Divine Grace
A.C. Bhaktivedanta Swami Prabhupada
tkg/

Dear Visvakarma,
Please accept my humble obeisances. All glories to Srila Prabhupada.
I have been instructed by Srila Prabhupada to reply to your letter of October 4.
Your sentiments were well expressed. As you wrote, Srila Prabhupada has taught us EVERYTHING, and before him we knew NOTHING. So our lamentation if he departs from this world is very real. You have well expressed all our feelings—that we will be lost without him. There is mention in the Krishna book that the great souls cross over the ocean of nescience but leave behind a boat for others to use in crossing. That boat is the Hare Krishna mantra and the mission of ISKCON. Actually it is not a mere boat but a super ark, capable of holding all the living entities in the universe. Either in his physical presence, or eventually without it, let us push on this movement of becoming devotees and making devotees.
Yesterday a letter came from Subhavilas. We are awaiting pictures of the Toronto Rathayatra. Everything there sounds like it is booming and very auspicious. Krishna must be cooperating with you because you are so sincere and hard working. if Srila Prabhupada recovers a little we can read him the recent news from Toronto.
Your servant,
Satsvarupa das Gosvami
visiting secretary to Srila Prabhupada
