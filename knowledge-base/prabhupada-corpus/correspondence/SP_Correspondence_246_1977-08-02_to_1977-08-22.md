### Correspondence text

Dear Hrisikeshananda Prabhu,
Please accept my humble obeisances. Srila Prabhupada has received your letter dated 25th July, 1977 and has requested me to reply.
As you may know His Divine Grace has been very ill since Gaura Purnima. Practically He is invalid and just to go the latrine He requires the assistance of two persons. On His instruction we have contacted two astrologers, and they have recommended the wearing of blue sapphire of seven carats weight. It seems to be the arrangement of the Supreme Personality of Godhead, that you are engaged in the jewelry business. If it is possible for you to procure such a gem and send it to His Divine Grace here in Vrndavana, this could be very beneficial for His health. He is suffering from an acute case of dropsy, and now even His translation work has become affected.
Srila Prabhupada is pleased to know that you are chanting 16 rounds daily of Hare Krishna mahamantra. Please continue this chanting regularly and when your business is a little successful, try to use the money for spreading the Krishna consciousness movement.
I hope this meets you in good health.
Your servant,
Tamal Krishna Gosvami
Secretary to His Divine Grace
A.C. Bhaktivedanta Swami Prabhupada

Dear Mrs. Perlmutter,
Hare Krishna. I have received both your letters to Srila Prabhupada and to myself, dated 6th July, 1977.
I have read your Letter to: Srila Prabhupada, which he heard with great interest. When Srila Prabhupada heard you say, “I hope I won’t become known as that pesty Mrs. Perlmutter” His Divine Grace laughed and said, “No, you are a good mother and you have given birth to a good child. You are doing a great service to Krishna. Your child is blessed, you are blessed, and your whole family is blessed. A Vaisnava child can purify 14 generations in the past and 14 generations in the future, of his family members.”
Thank you very much for the enclosed copy of the Parents Newsletter, which Srila Prabhupada liked very much. Thank you also for the enclosed article from the N.Y. Times regarding the Moscow Book Fair, and your thoughtful suggestion that ISKCON should be represented. Our Bombay office of Bhaktivedanta Book Trust will be displaying Srila Prabhupada’s books at the Moscow Book Fair and their booth has already been confirmed.
I hope this finds you and your family in good health.
Yours sincerely,
Tamal Krishna Gosvami

### Correspondence text

My dear Bhagavan Prabhu,
Please accept my most humble obeisances. His Divine Grace Srila Prabhupada has instructed me to thank you for your letter dated 27th July, 1977 along with the enclosed BBT Trustees report.
Srila Prabhupada was very pleased to hear from you. When He heard how serious your illness was, even that you were thinking that you might die, He commented, “Same thing as I am thinking. Typhoid is a very serious disease. I am an old man and I may die at any moment, but you are a young man.” And then Srila Prabhupada was thinking how you must have been feeling and He felt very compassionate. When you describe how you were always thinking of His Divine Grace, Srila Prabhupada with His eyes closed simply shook His head and said, “Thank you very much.”
His Divine Grace was especially pleased to know how you are increasing the book printing and distribution: “Book printing and distribution is our life. You are all giving life to your spiritual master by this activity. I am very glad to know how you are increasing the book distribution so much in Europe. Yes, you can print the unabridged edition of Bhagavad-gita in three parts. Whatever is easier to distribute then do that. Somehow people must receive it. We want to hand over this knowledge to the people. Do it. It is a very nice idea. You have already gained some success by introducing the Krishna Trilogy, so there is precedent. You have got a very good field and now so many devotees are joining. The people of France and Italy and Holland are very receptive. I have been to Rome. Italy is very cultured. The most cultured centers in Europe are France and Italy. And now Haya Haya is preaching in Spain. Let him stick there. It is the correct field for him.”
As Srila Prabhupada heard your report, His Divine Grace remembered New Mayapur very fondly: “Is the New Mayapur palace maintained nicely? I like to live there. Especially my wonderful room. I like to live there. I like that New Mayapur facility. Nice vegetables, flowers, grains. This is very encouraging to have such a farm, where all problems are solved. You say that there is so much rain. That is very good. There is enough rain and people will be happy due to the sankirtana yajna. Repair all the old buildings and give all the new people who come ample accommodations. Yes, you can change to a suitable new place in Paris. We have got our real place at New Mayapur. So you can move to wherever you can find a suitable place in Paris. The present building is not good but the location is very prominent.”
In this way His Divine Grace remembered with great pleasure your goodself and all of the nice devotees of New Mayapur.
Srila Prabhupada’s health has taken a slight turn for the worse. He has not eaten practically in one month, except for taking milk. So now He has become very very weak. He has not translated for the past week. The swelling has gone away due to the treatment of Kaviraj who is now going to attempt to revive Srila Prabhupada’s taste or appetite, which is the actual problem. Please have all the devotees there pray for Prabhupada’s recovery. Srila Prabhupada said that in our daily routine we can pray to Lord Krishna, “My dear Lord Krishna, if You desire, please cure Srila Prabhupada.”
Your servant,
Tamal Krishna Gosvami
Secretary to Srila Prabhupada
His Grace Bhagavan das Adhikari
c/o ISKCON New Mayapur

### Correspondence text

My dear Satsvarupa Maharaja,
Please accept my humble obeisances at your feet. I have been instructed by His Divine Grace Srila Prabhupada to reply your letter dated July 25th, 1977.
Srila Prabhupada explained the difference between first and second initiation as follows: “Why do you believe in rumors, that first initiation is not so important as second? I have already said that it is equally important, but you say rumor. Actually first initiation is more important. You can go without second initiation; if the first initiation is executed very thoroughly that is sufficient. First initiation stands strong. The spiritual master accepts the disciple’s sinful reactions upon giving first initiation. The Vedic system was to give the sacred thread at the first initiation. We are following Pancaratriki. Vedic initiation was given to a person born to a brahmana. That is not possible in this age. Therefore he has to be prepared by Hari Nam initiation and then second initiation. He is given a chance. Therefore others protest that I am giving initiation: He is not born of a brahmana, how can he be initiated?”
Srila Prabhupada listened to your description of how the Library Party will expand in the fall to South America, as well as the ideas which you are having for increasing the distribution in the U.S. When I heard about the slide show packages, such as the one about Jagannatha Puri Rathayatra, I immediately felt that slides of our Rathayatras throughout the world should also be included. Similarly in the slide show about Vrndavana there should be sufficient slides about our temple in Vrndavana also. Otherwise we are giving advertisements to places and temples which will automatically attract people to go there. However as you will see from my description in the next paragraph, going to these places does not automatically ensure that one will become so much advanced as they would were they to go to an ISKCON temple and participate in ISKCON activities. We should not waste our valuable energy distributing other’s books, or in this case advertising for others. Our intention is not to make money, but to increase the number of pure devotees and that is actually preaching. So I would caution that we be very selective in what the Library Party men sell, since they are such first-class preachers they should preach the highest truth.
Srila Prabhupada was very pleased to hear the description of the Rathayatra festival held in San Francisco. His Divine Grace could understand that the festival was held in as opulent and grand a style as it was in previous years when He was personally present. When He heard that $1500 worth of books was sold at the booth, He remarked, “This is very good. It is a sign that there is an actual demand. Please accept my humble obeisances at your feet. I beg to acknowledge receipt of your letter dated August 3rd, 1977.
You will be amazed to know, or perhaps you have already heard, that it is almost definite that Srila Prabhupada is coming to the West immediately. Throughout the world, all devotees are praying, “My dear Lord Krishna, if You desire, please cure Srila Prabhupada.” The love of His disciples is the greatest medicine and is having the most wonderful effect on His Divine Grace. Although physically He is in very poor health, He is being kept alive by the love of His devotees. In one letter received from Panca Dravida Maharaja, Panca Dravida offers his youth in exchange for Prabhupada’s old age. Srila Prabhupada upon hearing this said. “I have no old age. We are of the same age. It is not depending on old or young body—the atma has no old age. We are all Vaikuntha men. All new boys—nava yauvana.” In all of the letters which are daily received, the devotees are all praying to Prabhupada to please remain with us. Srila Prabhupada said after hearing one of these prayers, “I think I will have to stay on your request. Krishna is very kind—He is bhakta-vatsala. So many devotees are praying, their prayers cannot be frustrated.” Although the traveling will be very difficult upon Srila Prabhupada, He is requesting me now daily to take Him under any condition to the West. Srila Prabhupada said “I want to go. Can you arrange to carry me. I want to go where there is a cool climate. The worst thing is death. And I am not afraid of death. Here I feel like I am dying bit by bit. I have tried so many treatments for six months, but none have worked. Let me go to the West. Instead of no change, let there be change, either good or bad. Outside India if somehow good comes, otherwise here there is only bad. Somehow or other take me. I don’t expect any good staying here. I have psychological enthusiasm for going to the West. Do not be afraid. I am not afraid. Even if I die in any of our Temples, here or there, it is all Vaikuntha.” Later, Srila Prabhupada said, “I plan to stay in America. I will not come back until completing the Bhagavatam. I want to organize there. The Western boys are so nice. If I make everything strong, then the Movement will endure.” In a Letter to: day Srila Prabhupada wrote, “I think for this reason I am feeling so much inspiration to go on. In this condition no one thinks to go out. In this condition one prepares for death and I am going for touring. Actually I think Krishna is now present in this Krishna consciousness movement. Why should I be restricted to remaining in one place? So many temples, and they are all Vaikuntha.” Now more than ever Prabhupada wants to be surrounded by His beloved disciples. Prabhupada said to us, “Sit down. I want simply to see you all. It gives me vital force.” Gradually we all began to render service to His Divine Grace, Satsvarupa, Sruta Kirti, and Upendra massages His legs and lotus feet. I massaged His head, and Guru Krpa fanned and kept the air filled with fragrant incense. Prabhupada lay with His eyes closed very peacefully with us all attending to Him. Then He opened His eyes and said, “You all Vaisnavas, be merciful to me.”

### Correspondence text

Dear Pancadravida Maharaja,
Please accept my humble obeisances at your feet. I have been instructed by His Divine Grace Srila Prabhupada to thank you for your wonderful report dated 5th August, 1977.
In Srila Prabhupada’s room listening to your report along with His Divine Grace were His Holiness Satsvarupa Gosvami, Guru Krpa Swami, and Madhudvisa (who has just returned). We all, especially Srila Prabhupada, very much enjoyed hearing your report. Your opening prayers for His Divine Grace’s health and continued presence on this planet was taken with great feeling by Srila Prabhupada, who said, “I think I will have to stay on your request. Krishna is very kind. He is bhakta-vatsala. So many devotees are praying, and their prayers cannot be frustrated.” When you expressed your desire to exchange your youth with Srila Prabhupada’s old age, His Divine Grace said, “I have no old age. We are of the same age. It is not dependent on old or young body–the atma has no age. We are all Vaikuntha men. All new boys–nava yauvana.” It is due to such wonderful prayers like yours that Srila Prabhupada is now thinking to return to the West. His Divine Grace said, “I think for this reason I am feeling so much inspiration to go out. In this condition no one thinks to go out. In this condition one prepares for death and I am going for touring. Actually I think Krishna is now present in this Krishna consciousness movement. Why should I be restricted on one place? So many temples and they are all Vaikuntha.” You prayed for Prabhupada’s long life but His Divine Grace blessed you by saying, “May Krishna give you long life and preach Krishna consciousness. You are our future hope. Do this bidding.”
His Divine Grace very much appreciated the efforts being made in Guadalajara for a successful Rathayatra. He said, “Hmmm, Jagannatha is conquering!” When you described how the authorities are giving all facilities Srila Prabhupada said, “O this is wonderful. The mlecchas and yavanas instead of obstructing are giving all facilities. How Krishna is helping us.” Srila Prabhupada suggested that in future Rathayatras you can get one of the Deities being manufactured by Baradvaja to be placed on a Vyasasana at the Rathayatra festival. These Deities may also be placed on the Vyasasana permanently within your temple.
Prabhupada laughed very heartily when you described your relationship with Narayan Das as “giving the devil his due.” His Divine Grace commented, “This is good sense. When(?) one finds in the devil a divine spirit this is Vaikuntha consciousness. He does not find only bad.” Prabhupada, after rearing your description of the Colombians, added “cultural thieves.”
You are most welcome to come and be with Prabhupada in October. It does not matter that Hari Sauri may also be coming. Srila Prabhupada has said in the past that He can have 30 secretaries with Him at once and engage all of them. But anyway Srila Prabhupada said regarding your coming to see Him, “Most probably I am going to meet you very soon. This means that Srila Prabhupada may very possibly in America in October. His Divine Grace is not well at all but feels that by being with His beloved devotees in the West He will regain His life.
Hoping this meets you in the best of health and Krishna consciousness.
Tamal Krishna Gosvami
